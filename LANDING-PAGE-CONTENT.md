**Source:** `requirment/ShipGo_V1_Production_Scope_2.pdf` (rev 3) + `requirment/ShipGo_V1_Functional_Requirements.pdf`
**Scope of this document:** marketing copy and structure for the public landing page only — not the app UI.
**Languages:** Arabic (default, RTL) + English. Every string below exists in both.

---

## 1. Positioning

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | نقلة · Naqla |
| **What it is** | On-demand delivery and freight for goods across Syria — intercity and inside the city, on one flow |
| **Founding route** | Damascus ⇄ Aleppo |
| **Service shape** | Direct delivery: one pickup → one drop-off, up to 20 packages in one order |
| **Who pays** | Always the sender. The receiver never pays and never sees a price |
| **What we charge for** | Transport only. Naqla does **not** collect the value of the goods (no COD in V1) |
| **Audience** | Individuals, shops, and businesses sending goods; plus drivers/owner-operators as a second audience |

**One-line positioning**

> EN — *Send anything across Syria: the right vehicle, a verified driver, a price you see before you commit, and proof on every package.*
> AR — *أرسل أي شيء داخل سوريا: المركبة المناسبة، سائق موثّق، سعر تراه قبل التأكيد، وإثبات لكل طرد.*

### 3.3 How it works — four steps

| Step | EN | AR |
|---|---|---|
| **1 · Describe the shipment** | Pickup and drop-off addresses, the receiver's phone, what you're sending, how many packages, weight and size — and at least one photo of the load. | عنوان الاستلام وعنوان التسليم، رقم المستلم، وصف البضاعة، عدد الطرود، الوزن والأبعاد — وصورة واحدة على الأقل للحمولة. |
| **2 · See the vehicle, the fee and the terms** | We recommend the vehicle. You see the itemised fee, the cancellation tiers and the liability cap — then you accept them. | نوصي لك بالمركبة، وتظهر لك تفاصيل الأجرة، ورسوم الإلغاء حسب المرحلة، وسقف التعويض — وتوافق عليها قبل الإرسال. |
| **3 · A driver accepts, then you pay** | Pay online with Sham Cash or Syriatel Cash, or hand the fee to the driver in cash at pickup. | بعد قبول السائق تدفع: إلكترونياً عبر شام كاش أو سيرياتيل كاش، أو نقداً للسائق عند الاستلام. |
| **4 · Track, hand over, get proof** | Follow the status and arrival window. The receiver opens a link and shows a QR — no app needed. You get a delivery photo and a receipt. | تابع الحالة ونافذة الوصول. المستلم يفتح رابطاً ويعرض رمز QR — دون تحميل أي تطبيق. وتصلك صورة التسليم والإيصال. |

| Component | EN | AR |
|---|---|---|
| Base fare | Fixed per vehicle type | مبلغ ثابت حسب نوع المركبة |
| Distance | Rate per kilometre of route | تسعيرة لكل كيلومتر من المسار |
| Intercity surcharge | Fixed per city pair, when the trip crosses cities | رسم إضافي ثابت لكل زوج مدن عند النقل بين المدن |

| EN | AR |
|---|---|

### 3.8 Protection, terms & refunds

**Headline**
EN — **Written down before you send, not argued about afterwards.**
AR — **مكتوبة قبل الإرسال، لا محلّ نقاش بعده.**

| EN | AR |
|---|---|
| **A published liability cap.** Every shipment carries a stated maximum compensation for loss or damage. You see it and accept it at booking. | **سقف تعويض معلن.** لكل شحنة حد أقصى محدّد للتعويض عن الفقدان أو الضرر، يظهر لك وتوافق عليه عند الطلب. |
| **Compensation is calculated, not negotiated.** It is the lower of your declared value and the published cap, decided from the pickup photo, the delivery photo, the scan record and the GPS trail. | **التعويض يُحتسب ولا يُتفاوض عليه.** وهو الأقل بين القيمة المصرّح بها وسقف التعويض المعلن، ويُحدَّد من صورة الاستلام وصورة التسليم وسجل المسح ومسار GPS. |
| **Refunds are automatic, not a support request.** Online payments return to the same number and provider; cash returns as in-app credit. | **الاسترداد تلقائي وليس طلب دعم.** المدفوعات الإلكترونية تعود إلى الرقم والمزوّد نفسه، والنقدي يعود كرصيد داخل التطبيق. |
| **If the failure is ours, you pay nothing.** No driver found, driver no-show, vehicle failure, or a cancellation by us — full refund, no charge. | **إذا كان الخلل من طرفنا فلن تدفع شيئاً.** عدم توفر سائق، أو عدم حضوره، أو عطل المركبة، أو إلغاء من طرفنا — استرداد كامل بلا أي رسوم. |
| **Cancellation costs what the driver has actually done.** Nothing before a driver accepts; a low tier once he commits; the trip fee once he is on the way. | **رسم الإلغاء يعادل ما أنجزه السائق فعلاً.** لا شيء قبل قبول السائق، ورسم منخفض بعد القبول، ورسم رحلة بعد انطلاقه. |

**Exclusions that must be stated plainly (not buried) / استثناءات يجب ذكرها بوضوح:**

| EN | AR |
|---|---|
| Fragile items sent without declaring fragile handling | البضائع القابلة للكسر المرسلة دون الإفصاح عن ذلك |
| Goods on an open-bed vehicle without confirming wrapping | البضائع على مركبة مكشوفة دون تأكيد التغليف |
| Goods taken from the driver anywhere other than the registered address | البضائع المستلمة من السائق في غير العنوان المسجّل |

### 3.9 If a delivery fails

EN — **Nothing gets stuck. You choose what happens next.**
AR — **لا شيء يبقى معلّقاً. أنت من يقرّر ما يحدث بعدها.**

| Option | EN | AR |
|---|---|---|
| **Re-attempt** | Another delivery attempt within the limit. A re-attempt fee applies. | محاولة تسليم أخرى ضمن الحد المسموح، مع رسم محاولة إضافية. |
| **Redirect** | A new address or a new recipient, authorised by you. Repriced if the distance changes materially. | عنوان جديد أو مستلم جديد بتفويض منك، مع إعادة تسعير إن تغيّرت المسافة بشكل جوهري. |
| **Return** | The goods come back to you. A return leg fee applies and you confirm receipt with a photo. | تعود البضاعة إليك، مع رسم رحلة إرجاع وتأكيد استلام بصورة. |
| **Holding point** | If you can't take the goods back, they go to our holding point in the city. Daily storage applies, and the collection address and final date are stated to you in writing. | إن تعذّر استلامك للبضاعة تُنقل إلى نقطة الحفظ في المدينة، مع رسم تخزين يومي، ويُبلَّغ لك عنوان الاستلام والتاريخ النهائي كتابةً. |

**Set expectation:**
EN — *A delivery is all or nothing. If the receiver refuses one package, the whole order goes on hold and you decide.*
AR — *التسليم كامل أو لا شيء. إذا رفض المستلم طرداً واحداً يُعلَّق الطلب بأكمله وتعود القرار إليك.*

## 4. Bilingual & RTL rules for the landing page itself

These come straight from the localisation requirements and apply to the marketing site, not just the apps:

- Arabic is the **default** language; English is a switch, not the primary.
- Full RTL for Arabic: mirrored navigation, mirrored icons, mirrored progress and step indicators, right-aligned lists.
- Every string is localised — including buttons, form errors, empty states, cookie/consent text and 404. No raw English fallback ever reaches an Arabic visitor.
- Arabic script preserved intact in names and addresses; watch mixed-direction strings (a Latin plate number or a Latin brand name inside Arabic text).
- Numerals, dates and currency formatted per language, with correct currency placement.
- The Arabic build must be walked end to end by a native speaker before launch — not translated and shipped. RTL breaks show up in use: truncated buttons, arrows pointing the wrong way.

## 5. Claims we must NOT make

Each of these is either explicitly out of V1 or explicitly contradicted by the scope. Putting them on the page creates refunds, disputes and support load.

| Don't say | Why |
|---|---|
| "Cash on delivery" / "we collect payment for your goods" | COD was removed by decision. We charge transport only and never ask the receiver for money. |
| "A vehicle dedicated to your shipment" | A vehicle may carry several customers' orders on one trip. The correct positioning is **direct delivery with no scheduled wait** — exclusivity would be a separate premium product. |
| "Live GPS tracking the whole way" | Live position is shown on the **final approach** only. Before that: status + arrival window. |
| "Guaranteed delivery in X hours" | There are no learned ETAs at launch. Promise an arrival window, never a guaranteed duration. |
| "Multi-stop routes" / "several drop-offs in one order" | One pickup → one drop-off in V1. |
| "Partial delivery" / "the receiver can take some packages" | All or nothing. Refusing one package puts the whole order on hold. |
| "Insured shipments" / "full-value insurance" | Paid declared-value insurance is deferred. V1 has a **liability cap**, which is a different promise — say cap, not insurance. |
| "Scheduled routes / daily departures" | Shared and scheduled service is out of V1. |
| "Download the app to receive your delivery" | There is no receiver app. The receiver uses a browser link. |
| Any specific driver-share %, cap amount, storage rate or cancellation % | All are admin-configurable placeholders. See section 6. |
