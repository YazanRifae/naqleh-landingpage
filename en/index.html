How it works

Four steps from order to proof

  1. 1

    Describe the shipment

    Pickup and drop-off addresses, the receiver's phone, what you're sending, how many packages, weight and size — and at least one photo of the load.

  2. 2

    See the vehicle, the fee and the terms

    We recommend the vehicle. You see the itemised fee, the cancellation tiers and the liability cap — then you accept them.

  3. 3

    A driver accepts, then you pay

    Pay online with Sham Cash or Syriatel Cash, or hand the fee to the driver in cash at pickup.

  4. 4

    Track, hand over, get proof

    Follow the status and arrival window. The receiver opens a link and shows a QR — no app needed. You get a delivery photo and a receipt.

Photos are mandatory at booking. They are the baseline for any damage claim later.

The limits

What we carry — and what we don't

We carry

  • Documents and envelopes
  • General goods
  • Sealed, non-perishable food
  • Sealed medicine
  • Appliances and electronics
  • Furniture
  • Construction materials
  • Plants
  • Fragile items and glass
  • Palletised freight

We cannot carry

  • Hazardous materials — flammable, explosive, corrosive, compressed gas, toxic
  • Weapons, ammunition and their components
  • Live animals
  • Cash, gold, jewellery and bearer instruments
  • Unsealed or temperature-dependent food — no cold chain
  • Human remains or biological samples
  • Anything unlawful to transport
  • Sealed containers whose contents you won't declare

A driver may refuse prohibited goods at pickup. The refusal is recorded with a photo.

Protection & terms

Written down before you send, not argued about afterwards

  • A published liability cap

    Every shipment carries a stated maximum compensation for loss or damage. You see it and accept it at booking.

  • Compensation is calculated, not negotiated

    It is the lower of your declared value and the published cap, decided from the pickup photo, the delivery photo, the scan record and the GPS trail.

  • Refunds are automatic, not a support request

    Online payments return to the same number and provider; cash returns as in-app credit.

  • If the failure is ours, you pay nothing

    No driver found, driver no-show, vehicle failure, or a cancellation by us — full refund, no charge.

  • Cancellation costs what the driver has actually done

    Nothing before a driver accepts; a low tier once he commits; the trip fee once he is on the way.

Exclusions we state plainly

  • Fragile items sent without declaring fragile handling
  • Goods on an open-bed vehicle without confirming wrapping
  • Goods taken from the driver anywhere other than the registered address
  • Consequential loss of any kind
If a delivery fails

Nothing gets stuck. You choose what happens next.

  • Re-attempt

    Another delivery attempt within the limit. A re-attempt fee applies.

  • Redirect

    A new address or a new recipient, authorised by you. Repriced if the distance changes materially.

  • Return

    The goods come back to you. A return leg fee applies and you confirm receipt with a photo.

  • Holding point

    If you can't take the goods back, they go to our holding point in the city. Daily storage applies, and the collection address and final date are stated to you in writing.

A delivery is all or nothing. If the receiver refuses one package, the whole order goes on hold and you decide.

FAQ

The questions asked before a first shipment

When do I pay?

After a driver accepts your order. Nothing is charged while it is still looking for a driver.

Can the receiver pay?

No. The sender always pays, and the receiver is never shown a price.

Do you collect the price of the goods from the receiver?

No. We charge for transport only.

Does the receiver need the app?

No. A link arrives by SMS and the verification QR opens in any browser.

Can I choose the vehicle myself?

We recommend it from your load. You can upgrade to a larger one; we don't offer a smaller one than the load needs.

Why are photos mandatory?

They are the agreed starting condition of your goods, and they settle damage claims fairly.

What if the receiver isn't there?

The driver waits, records the failure with a photo, and you choose: re-attempt, redirect, or return.

What if my goods are damaged?

We compare the pickup and delivery photos and the scan record, and compensate per the published policy — the lower of your declared value and the liability cap.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Free before a driver accepts; after that the fee reflects the work already done.

Is the app in Arabic?

Arabic is the default, with full right-to-left design. English is one tap away.
